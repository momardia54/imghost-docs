# Organizing the library

## Views

The sidebar has two fixed views and your folders:

- **All images**: every image in the library.
- **Unsorted**: images that are not in any folder. Uploads from *All images* or *Unsorted* land here.
- **Folders**: a folder's view includes the images of all its sub-folders, and its sub-folders appear as cards above the images. The count next to each folder includes its sub-folders too.

The storage meter at the bottom of the sidebar shows the library's total size against the 10 GB of R2's free tier.

## Folders

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Create a top-level folder | The folder icon next to **Folders** |
| Create a sub-folder | Hover over the folder in the sidebar, then **+** |
| Rename | Hover, then the pencil; or double-click the name; or select the row and press F2 |
| Delete | Hover, then the bin |
| Expand or collapse | The arrow before the name, or the Left and Right arrow keys |

Deleting a folder deletes **everything inside it**: its sub-folders, their images, and the images' variants. The confirmation says how many images and sub-folders that is. Public links to those images stop working.

## Finding images

- **Search** (top bar, or press `/` or Cmd+K): matches image file names across the whole library, and folder names. Matching folders appear as cards with their path.
- **Sort**: newest first, oldest first, name A to Z, or largest first.
- **Grid or list**: the grid shows thumbnails, with a slider for their size; the list shows name, folder, type, size and upload date.

Sort, view and thumbnail size are remembered by your browser.

## Selecting

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Select one image | The circle in its top-left corner, or Cmd-click / Ctrl-click |
| Select a range | Select one, then Shift-click another |
| Select all loaded images | Cmd+A / Ctrl+A, or **Select all** in the selection bar |
| Clear the selection | Esc, or the × in the selection bar |

While a selection exists, clicking an image adds it to or removes it from the selection instead of opening it. The selection bar at the bottom offers **Copy links** (one per line), **Move** and **Delete**.

![Three images selected, with the selection bar](/screenshots/gallery-light.jpg)

## Moving

- Drag images (or a selection) onto a folder in the sidebar, onto a folder card, or onto **Unsorted**.
- Or select them and choose **Move**, then pick the destination. The dialog has a search box for large folder trees; double-click a folder to move straight away.

Moving never changes an image's link.

## Deleting

Select images and choose **Delete**, press Delete or Backspace, or use **Delete** in the lightbox. Deleting removes the original, its variants and its record. It cannot be undone, and its public link stops working. Browsers that already showed the image may keep showing it from their cache for a while; see [Links and sharing](/guide/sharing#caching).
